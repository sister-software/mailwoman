# Terrain Tiles (Tilezen Joerd) inputs: each publisher's own terms

- Retrieved: 2026-10-24
- Form: excerpts. Each section quotes the publisher's page through a fetch tool or a browser tab that returns page
  text. The full pages were not stored.

The regional inputs and their notices come from `international-terrain-tiles.md`, Tilezen's attribution document,
retrieved 2026-10-22. This file holds the terms each input's publisher states.

## EU-DEM: Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 1159/2013

- URL: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32013R1159
- Article 3: "Users shall have free, full and open access to GMES dedicated data and GMES service information under
  the conditions laid down in Articles 4 to 10, subject to the restrictions laid down in Articles 11 to 16."
- Article 7(1): "Access to GMES dedicated data and GMES service information shall be given for the purpose of the
  following use in so far as it is lawful: (a) reproduction; (b) distribution; (c) communication to the public;
  (d) adaptation, modification and combination with other data and information; (e) any combination of points (a)
  to (d)."
- Article 7(2): "GMES dedicated data and GMES service information may be used worldwide without limitations in time."
- Article 8(1): "When distributing or communicating GMES dedicated data and GMES service information to the public,
  users shall inform the public of the source of that data and information."
- Article 8(2): "Users shall make sure not to convey the impression to the public that the user's activities are
  officially endorsed by the Union."
- Article 8(3): "Where that data or information has been adapted or modified, the user shall clearly state this."
- Article 9: the data are provided "without any express or implied warranty".
- Recital 8: "Users should also be allowed to re-distribute GMES dedicated data and GMES service information, with
  or without modifications."

The Copernicus Land Monitoring Service data policy (https://land.copernicus.eu/en/data-policy) gives the
redistribution notice "European Union's Copernicus Land Monitoring Service information". The regulation states no
purpose limit, and states no commercial-use clause in either direction.
