# National Address Database (NAD) disclaimer — as retrieved

- Source: `https://www.transportation.gov/gis/national-address-database/national-address-database-nad-disclaimer`
- Retrieved: 2026-09-21
- Publisher: the United States Department of Transportation
- Version stated in the text: none. The page states it was last updated 2026-01-09.

The National Address Database supplies 85,482,142 of the 125,276,536 address points in the `us`
runtime bundle. That count is 68.2% of the bundle. The bundle's record mentioned neither the database nor its
publisher until this text was read. The rows reach the bundle through the Overture Maps Foundation.
Overture stamps each row `NAD`, so every row in the bundle identifies the publisher.

## What the disclaimer states about reuse, quoted

> The NAD is a work of the federal government and as such is not subject to copyright protection in
> accordance with 17 U.S.C. § 105. It is available for re-use without limitation or restriction.

## The restriction that is not a copyright restriction, quoted

> Although the NAD is open data, it is not intended for use as a mailing list and is subject to
> individual state statutes that prohibit it's use as a mailing list.

These two sentences appear on the same page, and a record that states only "no copyright under 17
U.S.C. § 105" would lose the second one. The first sentence states that copyright imposes no
restriction. The second states a restriction from another source on one particular use. That source is
individual state statutes, and the page neither names nor lists them. A postal address product is close to that
use, so the restriction belongs in the record and not in a footnote.

This text does not establish which states have those statutes, what they prohibit, or whether any of
them applies to what this repository does with the rows.

## What the disclaimer states about NAD's own sources

The disclaimer states that the National Address Database (NAD) is, with few exceptions, aggregated from state
data. The state data is in turn aggregated from local data.

The disclaimer also states that the states partnering with USDOT provide NAD data voluntarily and under no
obligation. It adds that not every contributing state has complete coverage.

NAD is therefore itself an aggregate. The federal-work statement covers NAD as published and does not
cover each contributing state's own publication.

## What this text does not settle

The text does not say which state or local body supplied a given NAD row, or what that body requires.
The bundle's rows have the source `overture:NAD` and record no finer source. This file elects no license.
