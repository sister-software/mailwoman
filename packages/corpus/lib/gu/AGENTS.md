# AGENTS.md — corpus sources for Guam

## How this repository elects address data

Guam takes the United States position in `../us/AGENTS.md`. Guam is an organized territory of the United States, and the Copyright Act's geographic definition of the United States includes "the organized territories under the jurisdiction of the United States Government" (17 U.S.C. § 101), so the Act's exclusion of facts (Feist Publications, Inc. v. Rural Telephone Service Co., 499 U.S. 340 (1991)) and its lack of a sui generis database right govern Guam as they govern a state.

A Government of Guam body's address file published without terms restricting its use is therefore elected with basis `other-documented`, citing Feist. Terms that spell out a restricted use remain a refusal.

The shared rules for reading a publisher's terms are in `packages/corpus/AGENTS.md` under "License election". This file states only the jurisdiction's legal position on address data. Those rules defer to it.
