# AGENTS.md — corpus sources for United Kingdom

## Electing address data

The United Kingdom keeps the database right of Directive 96/9/EC in domestic law through the Copyright and Rights in Databases Regulations 1997, unchanged by its withdrawal from the EU.

Royal Mail's Postcode Address File and HM Land Registry's Price Paid Data address fields are databases under that right, with terms that spell out restricted uses, and Price Paid Data is refused on those terms.

This repository has adopted no factual-data reading for the United Kingdom. Its rows are elected only on the publisher's own grant, read as `../../AGENTS.md` "License election" describes.

The shared rules for reading a publisher's terms are in `packages/corpus/AGENTS.md` under "License election". This file states only the country's legal position on address data, which those rules defer to.
