# AGENTS.md — corpus sources for Poland

## How this repository elects address data

Poland is an EU member state. Directive 96/9/EC gives the maker of a database a sui generis right over its contents when obtaining, verifying or presenting them took substantial investment. The maker's consent is required to extract or re-use a substantial part for 15 years from completion or substantial change. An address register is such a database, so its rows are elected only on the publisher's grant. Directive (EU) 2019/1024, Article 1(6), bars a public sector body from using that right to prevent re-use of documents within the directive's scope. Re-use still runs on the conditions or license the body attaches, so the grant is still what is elected.

This repository has adopted no factual-data reading for Poland. Its rows are elected only on the publisher's own grant, read as `../../AGENTS.md` "License election" describes.

The shared rules for reading a publisher's terms are in `packages/corpus/AGENTS.md` under "License election". This file states only the country's legal position on address data. Those rules defer to it.
