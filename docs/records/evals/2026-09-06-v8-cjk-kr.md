# 2026-09-06 — `v8-cjk-kr`: Korean under the CJK package, and the base swap

Record of the run that made Korean a served locale of `@mailwoman/neural-weights-cjk`, read against the pre-registration in
`corpus-python/src/mailwoman_train/configs/v8-cjk-kr.yaml`. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-06-kr-under-the-cjk-package.md`.
Issue: #2184. The kana-register base it replaces is recorded in `2026-09-06-v8-cjk-shared-head.md`.

## 1. The run

`v8-cjk-kr` seed 42 (Modal app `ap-zmeeIk4rQpF2FcBftt6JCW`), 24,000 steps, batch 256, bf16, cosine, from scratch: the
`v8-cjk-kana` recipe plus the Korean road-name-address corpus `v8-kr-2026-09-06` (2,000,000 train / 20,000 val / 20,000
board rows over 27 held-out 시군구, five registers, source `juso-kr`) at source share 0.38, the vocabulary re-sealed over
the union (3,165 entries). No new tag: 시/도 → `region`, 시군구 → `subregion`, 읍면동 → `dependent_locality`, 도로명 → `street`,
건물번호 → `house_number`, 우편번호 → `postcode`. The 2,000-step probe (`v8-cjk-kr-probe`) cleared its bar first: street
0.9729, house_number 0.9998, JP unmoved. Final val loss 0.7040, val macro F1 0.9983.

## 4. Served

Export: `export_to_onnx(max_length=96, char_window=7)`, opset 18 (the exporter's floor), 30.8 MB, PyTorch↔ONNX
max_abs_diff 6.2e-6 over 256 rows (128 JP + 128 KR). `release.config.json` `charWeights.cjk` names
`models/v8-cjk-kr-s42/served-package`; the workspace card is 0.0.2 (`files_md5` model
`7a24dba39ee8b0a26aef62162b9fad3e`, vocabulary `e7ada09fe92d0c0fc480f748528d580c`), staged at `cjk/v0.0.2/`.

- JP served read, 2,000 rows seed 42 through the resolver on the promoted candidate table: **1,924 accepted @15 km
  (96.2%)**, 573 within 1 km, against 1,925 / 574 on the kana base.
- `서울특별시 종로구 자하문로 104 (청운동)` with `--locale ko-KR`: region `서울특별시`, subregion `종로구`, dependent_locality
  `청운동`, street `자하문로`, house_number `104`.
- `富山県中新川郡上市町大岩148-7` with `--locale ja-JP`: prefecture `富山県`, municipality `中新川郡上市町` (the register repair),
  district `大岩`, house_number `148-7`; resolves Kamiichi (`wof:890519523`).
- Without `--locale`, a CJK input reached the Latin model at the time of the swap (`부산광역시 해운대구 반송로 910-1` →
  locality `부산광역시`, street `해운대구 반송로`; the JP row → locality `富山県中新川郡上市町大岩148`). Closed the same
  evening by the script-routed classifier (`ScriptRoutedClassifier` in `@mailwoman/neural`): the served entry points
  route a kanji or Hangul line to the CJK family per input, and the session withholds the locale's inferred country
  scope for a routed input. Bare `富山県中新川郡上市町大岩148-7` then parses whole and resolves Kamiichi at 4.9 km; the
  full 675-row board moved 0 non-CJK rows (`mwdev_compare` run `ffd9b114-6a62-48d5-b97f-3a5cdf983e21`).
