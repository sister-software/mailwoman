Record of the run that re-sourced Korean from the ministry's own 주소DB, added Taiwan as a served locale of
`@mailwoman/neural-weights-cjk`, and trained on typed business addresses from three government registries aligned
against the LABEL corpora. Read against the pre-registration in
`corpus-python/src/mailwoman_train/configs/v8-cjk-regs.yaml`. Issue: #2204. The base it replaces is recorded in
`2026-09-06-v8-cjk-kr.md`.

## 3. One instrument defect

The probe's first KR `dependent_locality` read was 0.6288 while the model labeled the rows correctly. The board puts
two `dependent_locality` spans on 5,894 of its 20,000 rows (읍/면 plus the 리 below it, or the road form's
parenthetical 동), and the scorer built one gold surface per tag (the last span) against one predicted surface (the
first run), so those rows could never hit. `decode_all_spans` now scores every gold span against every predicted run
of its tag; the centroid key still reads the first span on both sides (bdb03334f, test pinned). The control's KR
numbers above are re-scored on the fixed scorer in the same session.
