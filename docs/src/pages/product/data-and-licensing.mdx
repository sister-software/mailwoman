# Data, licensing, and security reports

This page records sources checked on 10 November 2026 for published `mailwoman@11.0.0`. The software license, a model's training provenance, and a downloaded geographic database have separate rights records. A recorded license expression identifies source terms. It does not decide every obligation for a particular redistribution or hosted use.

## Software and model artifacts

The published `mailwoman@11.0.0`, `@mailwoman/neural@11.0.0`, and `@mailwoman/neural-weights-en-us@11.0.0` manifests declare `AGPL-3.0-only OR LicenseRef-Commercial`. The repository's [license text](https://github.com/sister-software/mailwoman/blob/v11.0.0/LICENSE.md) offers AGPL-3.0-only use or a separate commercial agreement. The AGPL text includes source-disclosure conditions for covered network use. The repository's [commercial license template](https://github.com/sister-software/mailwoman/blob/v11.0.0/COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md) grants no rights until a specific agreement is executed. Read the [current license page](/license) and the applicable executed agreement for your use.

The commercial template limits its grant to code and model artifacts the licensor has authority to license. It states that third-party components and separately downloaded reference databases keep their upstream terms. The published en-US weights package includes [LICENSE.md](https://github.com/sister-software/mailwoman/blob/v11.0.0/packages/neural-weights-en-us/LICENSE.md) and [PROVENANCE.json](https://github.com/sister-software/mailwoman/blob/v11.0.0/packages/neural-weights-en-us/PROVENANCE.json). Its provenance record identifies OpenStreetMap-derived training inputs and their attribution. The presence of a training notice does not, by itself, establish the license of every later prediction or customer database. That application-specific interpretation needs owner or legal review.

The site publishes versioned [SPDX 2.3](/sbom/mailwoman-11.0.0.spdx.json) and [CycloneDX](/sbom/mailwoman-11.0.0.cdx.json) software bills of materials for `mailwoman@11.0.0`, plus a [CycloneDX data bill of materials](/sbom/mailwoman-data-11.0.0.cdx.json). Check the version and component identity in each file before using it for an inventory. The [packages and runtime reference](/docs/reference/packages-and-runtime) identifies the directly installed packages for the checked library path.

## Read the source publishers' terms

The following publisher pages were checked on 10 November 2026. They describe their own data and may describe a later release than the paths above.

- [OpenStreetMap's copyright page](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) identifies ODbL data. It requires credit to OpenStreetMap contributors and a clear notice that the data is available under ODbL when the data is used. It also describes the same-license condition for altered or built-upon data. The `candidate` bundle records an OpenStreetMap-derived Northern Ireland postcode fold. A separately built OpenStreetMap address-point database is another ODbL data path. Keep the applicable data artifact and its notice identifiable, and obtain owner or legal review of a proposed redistribution.
- [Who's On First's license page](https://www.whosonfirst.org/docs/licenses/) requires a link to its license and lists other sources that may have their own terms. The `candidate` bundle uses Who's On First place data. The registry has not traced each candidate name back to an upstream source.
- [GeoNames' publication page](https://www.geonames.org/about.html) describes its downloadable data as Creative Commons Attribution. The published registry records CC-BY-4.0 for the `candidate` bundle's GeoNames contribution. [Creative Commons' CC BY 4.0 terms](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) describe credit, license-link, and change-marking conditions when material is shared.
- [Overture's attribution page](https://docs.overturemaps.org/attribution/) lists terms by theme and source. The published registry assigns ODbL to Divisions in `candidate` and CDLA-Permissive-2.0 to its pinned `poi` build. Overture's current Places section lists source-level CDLA and Apache terms. Review the pinned artifact's source records before treating either summary as its complete notice.
- [Base Adresse Nationale's data page](https://adresse.data.gouv.fr/contenu-de-la-ban) states that its data is available under an open license. The published `fr` registry entry identifies the elected Licence Ouverte 2.0 path. Review the [recorded license text](https://github.com/sister-software/mailwoman/blob/v11.0.0/packages/corpus/data/licenses/licence-ouverte-2.0.md) and the artifact's source date for attribution.

The `us` bundle also draws on the United States Department of Transportation's National Address Database and Census TIGER data. The registry records that source bodies and permitted uses need further review, including its warning about mailing-list use. The [Department of Transportation's database page](https://www.transportation.gov/gis/national-address-database) identifies the database and its contributing levels of government. A public-domain statement about federal work does not resolve the terms of each state or local contribution in the bundle.

For a particular installation, inspect the selected bundle and its source records before publishing an attribution notice or redistributing data. The rights owner or legal reviewer needs to resolve the `candidate`, `fr`, and `us` questions above against the exact artifact and intended use. [Limits and release status](/product/limits-and-status) distinguishes a bundle listed in the registry from one installed in an environment.

## Report a security issue privately

The repository's [security policy](https://github.com/sister-software/mailwoman/blob/v11.0.0/SECURITY.md) directs vulnerability reports to **teffen@sister.software**. Include the issue's impact, reproduction steps or a proof of concept, and the affected version or commit. Use the [public problem-report guide](/docs/guides/report-a-problem) for a parsing quality or documentation issue. Keep exploit details out of a public GitHub issue.
