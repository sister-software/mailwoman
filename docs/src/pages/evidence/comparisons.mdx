# Compare Mailwoman with other geocoders

This page compares Mailwoman with three alternatives: Google's Geocoding API, a self-hosted Nominatim server, and Pelias with libpostal. It covers where the data lives, what running each one requires, what you may keep from its results, and the one measured comparison this project has published. Third-party facts below were read from each project's own documentation on 11 November 2026. Check the linked pages before deciding, because their terms and requirements change.

Mailwoman also serves Nominatim-, Photon- and libpostal-compatible APIs, so a client built for one of those can call it. [Point a compatible client at Mailwoman](/docs/guides/use-a-compatible-http-client) lists the fields those APIs return and the ones they lack.

## The measured comparison

The project has published one comparison that ran Mailwoman beside other geocoders: the [open-truth three-arm report of 18 August 2026](https://github.com/sister-software/mailwoman/blob/v11.0.0/docs/records/evals/competitive-parity/2026-08-18-open-truth-three-arm.md). It used Mailwoman model 9.1.0, and Pelias and Photon results captured in August 2026. Photon is a geocoder built on OpenStreetMap data. Google and Nominatim were not measured.

The [panel](/benchmarks/open-truth-panel.jsonl) has 297 addresses in six groups, each with a rooftop coordinate from an open address register. Every arm received the same 297 strings. The scorer counts a row as a hit when the returned coordinate lies within 1, 5 or 25 km of the reference, and counts a missing result as a miss at every distance.

| Arm             | Within 1 km        | Within 5 km        | Within 25 km       | No result |
| --------------- | ------------------ | ------------------ | ------------------ | --------- |
| Mailwoman 9.1.0 | 258 of 297 (86.9%) | 286 of 297 (96.3%) | 295 of 297 (99.3%) | 1         |
| Pelias          | 273 of 297 (91.9%) | 275 of 297 (92.6%) | 281 of 297 (94.6%) | 0         |
| Photon          | 255 of 297 (85.9%) | 273 of 297 (91.9%) | 276 of 297 (92.9%) | 20        |

The scorer's paired bootstrap (seed 20260807, 1,000 resamples) gives the Mailwoman − Pelias difference over all 297 rows:

- −5.1 percentage points at 1 km, 95% interval [−9.8, −0.7]
- +3.7 points at 5 km, [0.7, 6.7]
- +4.7 points at 25 km, [2.0, 7.4]

By group, the largest differences were in New Zealand, where Mailwoman led by 24.6 points at every distance over 57 rows, and in a mixed European group, where Mailwoman trailed by 42.2 points at 1 km over 45 rows.

Three limits apply to these numbers:

- They describe model 9.1.0 and the data of August 2026. Release 11.0.0 ships model 10.8.0 and has not been measured on this panel.
- Mailwoman and Pelias use some of the same open registers that supplied the reference coordinates. The distance partly measures how well each one retrieves that register's row.
- 297 rows in six groups describe those groups. They do not estimate the error rate for every address.

## Reproduce the comparison

The scorer recomputes every count above from the committed results file, with no network access. From a checkout of the repository at tag `v11.0.0`:

```bash
cd docs/static/benchmarks
node open-truth-three-arm.mjs
```

It prints the per-group table, the pooled table, the paired bootstrap and the rows where an arm returned no result. It was run again for this page and exited 0 with the counts shown. A fresh run of the three geocoders needs Pelias and Photon servers with their own data. This page does not cover setting them up.

The [benchmark index](/evidence/benchmarks) lists the other dated measurements and explains how to read each metric.
