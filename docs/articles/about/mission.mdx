# Mission

Addresses are infrastructure. A lookup should cost about as much as finding an address. You
should be able to run that lookup without anyone's permission. Mailwoman provides that capability
as software you run yourself.

## Why the engine is open

The parser, the geocoder, the resolvers, the drop-in servers and the published model weights are
AGPL-3.0. You can read every line, fork it, and run it in production without asking us. The AGPL
adds one condition. If you distribute software built on Mailwoman, or run it as a network service
that other people use, those users are entitled to your corresponding source under the same license.
Teams that cannot meet that condition can buy the [commercial license](../pricing.mdx), which
releases them from it.

Open source changes what a market charges for. When good editors became free, paid products moved to
work that an editor cannot do by itself. We think address parsing should be free. We intend to make
it free.

## Why we charge for data and licensing rather than for calls

The engine costs money to build once. The data takes ongoing work to keep current. Streets get
renamed, postcodes get reissued, and buildings get numbered, so a gazetteer that was right last year
is wrong this year in ways nobody announces. A subscription pays for that recurring work and for the
license release that some companies need.

For that reason the price does not depend on your volume. A signup form that re-parses on every
keystroke makes thousands of calls per user. In your own process those are function calls. They do
not appear on the invoice. We could not meter usage across your machines even if we wanted to,
because the software runs on them and does not report back.

## What we will not do

- **Put a key on the engine.** The open release contains the whole engine.
- **Instrument you.** The software has no telemetry, analytics or usage ping. You can verify this
  yourself: see [Security and compliance](./security-and-compliance.mdx).
- **Publish a number without the script that produced it.** Every accuracy figure we put out ships
  with its script, inputs and result file: see [Benchmarks](../resources/benchmarks/index.mdx).
- **Withdraw what we published.** Every AGPL release already shipped stays licensed to you
  permanently. That follows from the license. The license leaves each grant in force after publication.

## Who pays for it

Customers do. Subscriptions and enterprise work fund the engineering, so we answer to the people who
use the software. We do not sell advertising or data. We do not collect queries to train models.
The software runs on your machines, so your queries never reach us.
