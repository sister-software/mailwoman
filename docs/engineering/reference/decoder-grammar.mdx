# Decoder grammar contract

**Status.** Design record and standing contract, from the 2026-08-24 design session. It fixes four
things in one place: the objective the shipped decoder actually maximizes, the objective the failing
board rows imply we mean, a constraint taxonomy stated as **measurable predicates** rather than
intuitions, and the toponymy vocabulary the rest of the docs should use. Companions:
[`SCHEMA.mdx`](./SCHEMA.mdx) (the `ComponentTag` union and `PARENT_OF`),
[`2026-08-16-autodecoder-coherence.md`](../../records/research/2026-08-16-autodecoder-coherence.md)
(the resolver-side coherence ladder this contract mirrors on the parser side), and #727 (the span
head).

## Position: where the rules live

An explicit rules engine places every newly observed address format into a hand-written case. The
maintenance cost of that position is documented by every long-lived rules-based geocoder: the rule
base grows monotonically with observed formats, and each addition risks the previous ones. A pure
sequence labeler takes the opposite position: the rules are implicit in the weights, which removes
the case analysis and with it the ability to read, test, or falsify any single rule.

This contract takes a third position. **Structure is declared in the objective — the factorization
of the score and two small typed grammars carried as data. Content is learned.** A rule here is a
term in a score with a measurable firing census, never a case in code and never only a tendency in
the weights.

## 1. The grammar we decode today, exactly

The shipped model emits per-token scores over a BIO vocabulary (Begin/Inside/Outside markers over
`ComponentTag`). The decoder (`packages/neural/viterbi.ts`) solves:

```
ŷ = argmax over y ∈ L_BIO of   Σₖ e(k, yₖ)  +  Σₖ M[yₖ₋₁][yₖ]
```

- `e(k, yₖ)` — the **emission**: the model logit for label `yₖ` at token `k`, plus the soft priors
  that add into it: the anchor channel, the FST gazetteer prior, the country-surface clues, and the
  placetype-pair `parentDelta` with its header-baked β on an index hit.
- `M` — the transition matrix. In the shipped bundle this is the **structural mask only**: score 0
  for every legal BIO transition, −∞ for `O → I-X`, `B-X → I-Y (X ≠ Y)`, and start → `I-X`.
  Learned transitions are added only when a bundle ships them (`classifier.ts:126`); the shipped
  `en-us` overlay ships neither `crf-transitions.json` nor `semi-crf-transitions.json`
  (verified 2026-08-24).
- After the argmax, outside the objective: local repairs (word-consistency heal, postcode
  consistency), greedy overlap resolution (`resolve-proposal-overlaps.ts` — confidence descending,
  then shorter span first), tree assembly under `PARENT_OF` (`build-tree.ts`), and
  `validate-tree.ts`'s two checks (illegal-edge, stranded-dependent). These are checks and local
  rewrites. They cannot move the decode to a different global optimum.

**Consequence, provable from the form.** Every term has arity ≤ 2 over adjacent tokens, and the
arity-2 terms are 0/−∞ almost everywhere. Two parses that differ by re-labeling the tail of a
delimiter-free phrase — `street="Unter den Linden"` versus `street="Unter" + locality="den Linden"`
— differ in score only through the emissions on the affected tokens; the one transition that
changes is legal, score 0. No term in the objective represents _this phrase is one constituent_.
Phrase cohesion, segment structure, and cardinality exist in the system only to the degree the
encoder's contextual token emissions happen to encode them.

## 2. The grammar we mean

A parse is a triple `(S, T, H)`: a segmentation `S` into spans, types `T : S → ComponentTag`, and
typed headed edges `H` over the spans. The target objective:

```
score(S, T, H | x)  =  Σ_{(i,j,t) ∈ S} φ(i, j, t)   +   Σ_{(h,d,r) ∈ H} ψ(h, d, r)
```

maximized **jointly**. The stages of the intuitive pipeline — lexical cues, candidate spans,
protected entities, inter-span grammar, address semantics — are the _terms of one objective_, not
sequential decisions: the overlapping name candidates in
`Cafe at St Mary's, Church of St Mary the Virgin, St Mary's St, Shrewsbury SY1 1BX` cannot be
committed before the inter-span grammar runs, because which candidates are real is decided by which
segmentation makes the whole address cohere.

**Atomicity is a signature restriction, not a prohibition on internal grammar.** `φ(i, j, t)` may
read anything inside `x[i..j]`, internal syntax included. `ψ` reads spans only as atoms. No term
crosses a span boundary. A parser is free to know that `of` in `Port of Spain` is an adposition;
that knowledge raises `φ` for the whole span as a well-formed toponym and contributes nothing to any
attachment across its boundary.

**The dependency reading and the existing tree are the same object.** The `HEAD/REL` view — a
venue or street as root, `address`-typed edges to locality, postcode, region — is the `AddressTree`
under `PARENT_OF`, with `REL=address` equal to the resolver's containment set and
`number → street` an existing `PARENT_OF` edge. One production is currently missing from the
containment contract: a POI under a POI (`Cafe at St Mary's` located at
`Church of St Mary the Virgin`). `containment.ts:61` gives `venue` the parents
`["street", "locality"]` only; the Heathrow sub-venue case was handled by widening `unit`'s
parents, not by a venue→venue edge. Adding that production is schema work and is tracked here
rather than assumed.

## 3. Toponym-internal grammar, and the vocabulary for it

Standard toponymy decomposes a place name into:

- **specific element** — the distinctive name: `York`, `China`, `Spain`, `Gràcia`
- **generic element** — the feature or type term: `Republic`, `Mount`, `Lake`, `Port`, `Church`
- **modifier / qualifier** — `New`, `North`, `Greater`, `People's`

Under this vocabulary: `New York` is modifier + specific. `People's Republic of China` is
modifier + generic + `of` + specific — real internal grammatical structure, and **not** one opaque
prefix lexeme. `Port of Spain` is generic + `of` + specific. `Elephant and Castle Road` carries a
coordinator inside its specific element, which is exactly why a token-level reading misparses it as
a junction.

This internal grammar is a small typed grammar, `G_name`, and its place in the objective is fixed by
the atomicity restriction: **`G_name` scores the inside of `φ` and nothing else.** A span coheres as
a name partly _because_ its internal parse under `G_name` is well-formed — that is how an
out-of-registry pub name like `Ye Three Lords` earns protection from shape alone — and that
well-formedness never licenses an external attachment into the span's interior.

What exists today, mapped to this vocabulary: the street domain has real generic-element inventories
(the street-type lexicon, the street morphology FST, the `street_prefix`/`street_suffix` tags). The
toponym domain has none — no modifier inventory, no generic-element inventory for administrative
names. The FST gazetteer prior knows whole surfaces but applies them as per-token emission biases.

## 4. The greediness dial — current and target form

The question "where is a toponym's internal cohesion provided to the decoder — a boost, a
greediness dial?" has a precise current answer: **the dial exists, as scattered scalar constants
patching sub-span leakage.** `fst-prior.ts` discounts one- and two-token FST matches ("far likelier
to be coincidental than a three-token one", the #1142 length-scaling), and the pair-index β is a
second scoped constant. Both are global, hand-tuned, and act on symptoms of the same defect,
established next.

**Proposition (token factorization leaks registry evidence to sub-spans).** The country-surface
lexicon hits `Spain` inside `Port of Spain`. That evidence lands as an emission bias on the token.
The objective sums per-token terms, so the split parse (`locality="Port of" + country="Spain"`)
collects the bias as support for `B-country`, while the cohesive parse must overcome the same bias
arguing against `I-locality` on that token. A registry hit on a sub-span raises the wrong parse's
score mechanically. A length discount shrinks the subsidy; it cannot change its sign.

**Proposition (span factorization removes the defect by construction).** When evidence attaches to
span terms, a gazetteer entry for `Spain` contributes only to parses containing exactly the span
`(Spain, country)`, and the entry for `Port of Spain` contributes only to parses containing that
whole span. The split parse never collects the maximal entry's evidence. Registry maximality stops
being a constraint anyone enforces and becomes a corollary of where evidence may bind.

Under span factorization the greediness dial collapses to **one interpretable parameter**: the
weight of shape-based protection (`G_name` well-formedness in `φ`) relative to registry evidence —
the protection strength for names the gazetteer does not know. That parameter is calibratable and
D-rule-testable. The scattered discounts it replaces are the current system's implicit, global
setting of the same quantity.

## 5. Constraints as measurable predicates — not intuitions

The taxonomy below was **induced from the 19 board rows that regress under every candidate of the
v5.0.0 line** (see the [2026-08-23 retrospective](../../records/evals/retrospectives/2026-08-23-v500-from-scratch-base.md)).
Until each constraint exists as a detector with a measured census, it is a hypothesis. The standing
rule, mirrored from the coherence record's ladder: **no constraint graduates to a decode-time term
before it runs as a deterministic detector over the board, and its census is read** — rows it
explains, rows it would flag falsely. Detectors are graded against truth-graded failures, never
against the model's agreement with itself (the same echo-chamber rule the coherence record fixes
for the resolver-side checker).

| id  | predicate over `(x, parse)`                                                                                                                         | inputs required                             | status                                                                                                                                           |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| C1  | spans disjoint; uncovered tokens are O                                                                                                              | parse                                       | implemented (post-hoc, greedy)                                                                                                                   |
| C2  | tags nest as a tree under `PARENT_OF`                                                                                                               | parse, containment table                    | implemented (build + validator)                                                                                                                  |
| C3  | BIO well-formedness                                                                                                                                 | label sequence                              | implemented (structural mask)                                                                                                                    |
| C4  | a span boundary interior to a delimiter-free phrase is licensed by a delimiter, an affix lexeme, a character-class change, or a registry match edge | parse, phrase-grouper units, affix lexicons | **detector buildable today** — the grouper computes the units and does not bind the decode                                                       |
| C5  | the segment-label sequence belongs to the address system's template `G_c`                                                                           | parse, per-country templates                | **needs inventory** — `packages/codex/address-system-conventions.ts` is the natural home; templates not yet stated as machine-readable sequences |
| C6  | no sub-span registry hit licenses a boundary inside a span a longer registry entry covers                                                           | parse, FST maximal matches                  | **detector buildable today** — walk the FST for covering entries over every decoded boundary                                                     |

The proposed instrument is a constraint-violation census over the board — an extension of
`mwdev_diagnose`'s mechanism accounts — so that every board failure carries the predicate(s) it
violates, and the taxonomy is thereafter read from evaluation rather than argued from examples.

## 7. Empirical support

1. **The flipping rows are the rows the objective cannot separate.** The changed spans sit at
   0.4–0.6 shipped confidence (`Rua Augusta` 0.61, `Port of Spain` 0.47, `Andorra la Vella` 0.42).
   The 40 of 649 board rows that flip between v5.0.0 and v5.1.0 — two 60,000-step trainings
   differing in 2.23% of admitted rows — concentrate in this class.
2. **Training on instances of C4 moved only the taught surface family.** Raising the
   `synth-suffix-boundary` sampler weight 2.5 → 10.0 for 1,000 steps healed exactly
   `Passeig de Gràcia`, `Passeig de Sant Joan`, and the `Unit 1` swallow in
   `Somewhere In Bangkok Thai Restaurant, 1103 Queen St Unit 1, Southington, CT 06489` — 3 rows,
   attributed against a placebo run sharing base, seed, and steps — while `Unter den Linden`, the
   city splits, and the GB venue cluster (the same abstract constraint, other surface families)
   did not move. Emissions memorize constraint instances; the transfer radius is representational
   similarity, not the rule's scope.
3. **The data route also over-generalizes along the wrong axis.** The trailing-region corpus rows
   taught _text in the trailing position is administrative_; v4.8.0 applied that along the
   positional axis and destroyed venues (`Ye Three Lords, 27 Minories, London EC3N 1DE` →
   `locality="Ye Three Lords"`). Four dose adjustments could not separate the teaching from the
   damage, because both travel on the same representational axis.

## 8. Machinery map

| target-grammar object           | existing artifact                                                                | status                                      |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| span + type + confidence decode | the #727 span head; `semi-crf-transitions.json` parse path (`classifier.ts:132`) | trained experimentally; not shipped         |
| candidate spans                 | phrase-grouper (pipeline stage 2.7)                                              | exists; output does not bind the decode     |
| `HEAD/REL` tree                 | `AddressTree` + `PARENT_OF` (`build-tree.ts`, `containment.ts`)                  | built post-decode, unscored                 |
| POI-under-POI (`located_at`)    | none — `venue` parents are `["street", "locality"]`                              | schema decision open                        |
| `G_c` segment templates         | `packages/codex/address-system-conventions.ts`                                   | home exists; templates not machine-readable |
| `G_name` toponym grammar        | street domain only (street-type lexicon, morphology FST, affix tags)             | toponym modifier/generic inventories absent |
| registry evidence               | FST prior, country-surface clues, anchor channel                                 | present — token-granular, hence the §4 leak |

Every cue the target grammar consumes is already computed. The single delta is the **output space**:
per-token labels smear span-shaped evidence into token space, where §4 shows it can subsidize the
parses it should forbid.

## 9. Registered next measurements

1. **Constraint-violation census** (C4 and C6 detectors are buildable from existing artifacts):
   label every board failure with its violated predicates; read the census before any decode change.
2. **Ablation prediction**: suppressing the country-surface clue on tokens covered by a longer
   gazetteer locality entry flips `Port of Spain`-class rows without retraining. Expressible in the
   engine config; falsifies §4 if it fails.
3. **FST maximal-match check**: whether `fst-prior.ts` prefers covering entries over nested ones is
   UNKNOWN; read the code, record the answer here.
4. **Schema decision**: admit or reject the venue→venue containment edge for the POI-under-POI
   production, with a board row as its acceptance test.
