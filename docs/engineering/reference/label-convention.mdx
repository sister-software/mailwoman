# Label convention

This page states how an address's components are labeled where the written form leaves a choice. Every rule gives
the reading a person writing the address means, so a model is scored against the address and not against the
adapter that produced the row. An adapter that labels a form differently is wrong under this page. Correct the adapter
before you count a model's disagreement with it as a model error.

The rules use the tags of [`SCHEMA.mdx`](./SCHEMA.mdx). A field here is all spans of one tag joined in text
order, with `street_prefix`, `street` and `street_suffix` joined into one street, as the comparison battery scores
them (`packages/mailwoman/tools/eval-harness/battery/classify.ts`).

The hand-labeled disagreement rows in
`packages/mailwoman/tools/eval-harness/fixtures/label-convention/disagreement-labels-v1.jsonl` apply these rules, and
each row records the rule that decided it. `battery/hand-labels.ts` scores a battery report against them. The file is
hand-authored. Each of its 229 rows was read and labeled by hand: 183 keep their source labels, 41 are corrected and 5
are written. A scratch writer serialized the session's decisions. `readHandLabels` refuses a row that names no known
rule, lacks labels, or is corrected without keeping its source labels. No
committed tool regenerates the file, so a correction is an edit to the row.

## A city that shares its region's name

`Hamburg`, `Sevilla`, `Fier`, `Tacna`, `Hong Kong`, `Singapore`.

- A name written once, as the address's last place before any postcode or country, is the `locality`:
  `Altona, Hamburg` is `dependent_locality=Altona`, `locality=Hamburg`. `Calle Sierpes 12, 41004 Sevilla` is
  `locality=Sevilla`.
- The same name written a second time after the locality is the `region`:
  `Calle Sierpes 12, 41004 Sevilla, Sevilla` adds `region=Sevilla`.
- A name written after a different locality is the `region`, or the `country` for a territory that is one:
  `16B Man Tai Street, Hung Hom, Kowloon, Hong Kong` is `locality=Kowloon`, `region=Hong Kong`. A city-state written
  once is its own `locality`: `Guardian, 1, Fusionopolis Way, Singapore`.

Who's On First stores Hamburg and Sevilla as regions with a locality of the same name. The address writes the name
once, and the reader means the city.

## `LOT 38`

- After a house number, `LOT <n>` is a sub-premise, the `unit`: `439 MORROW RD LOT 142` is `house_number=439`,
  `street=MORROW RD`, `unit=LOT 142`. US mobile-home and trailer parks write this.
- In place of a house number, `LOT <n>` is the `house_number`, designator included: `LOT 38 Smith Road` is
  `house_number=LOT 38`. Australian and New Zealand rural parcels write this, and so do the Philippine block-and-lot
  forms (`Blk 12 Lot 16 Sec 18`).

## `# 200`

The number sign is a unit designator, with or without a space, as the only designator or after another one.

- `300 N WASHINGTON ST # 710` is `street=N WASHINGTON ST`, `unit=# 710`.
- `2331 MILL ROAD SUITE # 100` is `unit=SUITE # 100`, and `STE 111 # 216` is one `unit`.
- Before the street with no other number, `# 6` is the `house_number`: `# 6 EUREKA CIRCLE`.
- A number sign inside a business name stays in the venue: `John Chea & Sons Food Store #1`, `THE BOSTICK #1`.

## `P O BOX`

A post office box is a `po_box`, whatever its spacing or punctuation: `PO BOX 190`, `P O BOX 190`, `P.O. Box 190` and
`Post Office Box 190` are each `po_box`, the number included. It is never a `street`. A street and a box in one
address are labeled separately.

## A unit inside a venue name

A unit designator followed by its number, written after the occupant's name, is the `unit`:
`Dr. Carlos Días López Suite 102, 1449 Calle Américo Salas` is `venue=Dr. Carlos Días López`, `unit=Suite 102`.
A doubled designator stays in the unit (`Suite Suite 203`).

The occupant's own name keeps any number written in it (`Мактаби №25`, School No. 25). A name repeated after the street
is the venue again, and a facility name in the second address line is the venue (`610 East 24TH Street MEDICAL
CLINIC` is `venue=MEDICAL CLINIC`).

## A bare place between the number and the city

A name with no thoroughfare word, written between the house number and the locality, is decided by its words:

- A thoroughfare word makes it the `street`: `Sheshi Skënderbej` (square), `Calle`, `Rruga`, `улица`, `Street`.
- A settlement word makes it the `dependent_locality`: `Idogan Village`, `Richmond Estate`, `Purok 1`, Thailand's
  `Moo 5` (village number), `commune`, `barrio`, `colonia`.
- With neither, the source's own field decides: OSM `addr:place` or `addr:suburb` is the `dependent_locality`, and
  `addr:street` is the `street`. Senegal's `457, Parcelles Assainies, commune de parcelles assainies` is
  `dependent_locality=Parcelles Assainies`, because OSM tags the commune as a place.

## Values that are no component

A telephone number written where the house number goes is no component (`Sadik munshi, 037634557 Mohammed Bin
Khalifa St.`). A value in the house-number field that has the country's postcode form and stands before a place
name is the `postcode` (`SWEDISH CHURCH BRANCH, 35091 SHIWINGA`).
