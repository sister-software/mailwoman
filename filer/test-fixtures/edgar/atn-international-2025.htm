(6)Includes ten consolidated wholly owned subsidiaries also providing wireline, wireless, carrier and video services under the “One Communications” brand name in Bermuda and one wholly owned subsidiary providing wireline, carrier and video services under the “Logic” brand name in the Cayman Islands.
